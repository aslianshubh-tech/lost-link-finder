ABOUT THE PROJECT

A practical answer to a common campus problem.

LostLink is a student-friendly lost and found system built to make one small part of college life less frustrating.

01

The problem

Students often lose belongings on campus and there is no centralized system to connect lost and found reports. A message in a group chat can disappear before the right person sees it.

02

The solution

LostLink lets students report lost and found items, browse what is already on campus, and see possible connections. Its rule-based matching system checks category, name, color, location, date, time, and description.

03

The technology

PythonFlaskSQLiteHTMLCSSJavaScript

There is no AI or machine learning behind the matches. The score is intentionally transparent so students can understand why two reports were connected.
